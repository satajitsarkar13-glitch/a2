Cookie Policy

How HeartyMealStar utilizes browser cookies and local storage to enhance your culinary journey.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are compact alphanumeric text files downloaded to your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. Local storage (localStorage) is an analogous browser technology that enables websites to store and retrieve data locally on your device without transmitting data over the network with every server request.

These mechanisms allow HeartyMealStar to remember your interface preferences, sustain smooth site navigation, and understand how readers interact with our culinary guides.

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

We organize our cookies and storage tokens into three defined classifications:

Cookie Category Specific Identifier Purpose & Functionality Duration
Essential / Functional hearty_theme Stores your preferred UI display mode (Light or Dark theme). Persistent (1 Year)
Essential / Functional hearty_cookie_choice Remembers your cookie consent banner decision so you are not re-prompted. Persistent (1 Year)
Analytics & Telemetry _ga, _ga_* Google Analytics tokens used to compute aggregated, non-personally identifiable page visits. 2 Years

3. How We Do NOT Use Cookies

We take pride in maintaining an uncluttered, reader-focused culinary publication. We strictly enforce the following negative constraints:

  • We do NOT deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or behavioral profiling pixels.
  • We do NOT sell or license cookie telemetry to external data syndicates or marketing brokers.
  • We do NOT store sensitive personal identification, financial information, or raw passwords in browser cookies.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have complete authority over the storage of cookies on your device. You can configure your browser settings to reject all non-essential cookies, delete existing cookies, or notify you when a new cookie is issued.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary functional cookies (such as localStorage) may prevent certain interactive features—such as the Dark Mode toggle or Dinner Configurator state—from persisting between visits.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

HeartyMealStar may periodically revise this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our technology stack or legal requirements. We encourage readers to review this page periodically.
